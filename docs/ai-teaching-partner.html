AI as a Teaching Partner

A deeper-dive online book that expands the 2‑hour workshop with multidisciplinary examples, ready-to-use prompts, and complete teaching patterns.

AI as a Teaching Partner: Practical Applications for Multidisciplinary Business Education is an online book that takes the core ideas from this workshop – prompting as a foundation, AI in pedagogical design and delivery, critique, ethics, and assessment – and develops them in greater depth with discipline-specific examples.

Who the Book Is For

The book is written for staff in the School of Management and Marketing and colleagues in related business disciplines who want concrete, non‑technical ways to integrate AI into teaching.

  • Unit coordinators and lecturers designing AI‑aware units and assessments
  • Tutors, sessional staff, and teaching support who want ready‑to‑use prompts and activities
  • Program leaders and curriculum designers thinking about AI at course level
  • Researchers and HDR supervisors exploring appropriate AI use in research and supervision

How It Connects to the Workshop

Many chapters align directly with resources on this site:

  • Prompting as foundation: expands the ideas in the C.R.A.F.T. Prompting Framework, Seven Essential Prompt Techniques, and Prompt Chaining Techniques.
  • Critique and evaluation: develops the AI Critique Toolkit – Becoming a Smart Business Professional with more examples and assessment ideas.
  • Ethics, data governance & integrity: aligns with the Ethics, Data Governance & Integrity handout and the Assessment 2030 framing used in the workshop.
  • Design & delivery: provides extended patterns that build on AI in Pedagogical Design and Delivery and the main presentation.
  • Assessment and process: offers complete process‑over‑product assessment models and unit design templates that go beyond the 2‑hour session.

Multidisciplinary Examples

Each major chapter includes tabbed, discipline‑specific examples so you can see how the same pattern plays out in different areas, for example:

  • Business & Marketing – campaign design, segmentation, competitive analysis
  • Management & Organisation – change management, leadership scenarios, team dynamics
  • Human Resources – recruitment workflows, performance management, employee relations
  • Tourism & Hospitality – customer journeys, service recovery, revenue management
  • Supply Chain & Logistics – demand forecasting, supplier strategies, risk scenarios
  • Information Systems – requirements gathering, project planning, governance
  • Accounting & Finance – analysis, audit workflows, compliance scenarios
  • Economics – policy analysis, modelling, and scenario exploration

Ways You Might Use It

  • As a personal implementation guide after attending the workshop
  • As a source of copy‑paste prompts and activities for your units
  • As background reading when redesigning assessments or whole units
  • As a reference when talking with colleagues, program leaders, or accreditation bodies about AI literacy
