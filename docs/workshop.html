Workshop Structure

Duration: 2 hours | Format: Interactive presentation with hands-on activities

This workshop provides hands-on experience with AI tools for education. Each activity builds practical skills you can apply immediately in your teaching.

Workshop Activities

🔍

Welcome & Landscape

Quick overview of generative AI and its place in education

Duration: 15 min

⚙️

Automate the Tedious

Use AI to save time on repetitive tasks like grading, feedback, and administrative work

Duration: 20 min

✍️

Art of the Prompt

Master the CRAFT framework for writing effective prompts that get quality results

Duration: 25 min

✓

5-Step Critique

Quality control: Learn to evaluate AI output critically using a systematic framework

Duration: 20 min

📋

Assessment Stress-Test

Test your assessments against AI tools to identify vulnerabilities and redesign opportunities

Duration: 25 min

🛡️

Secure & Sustainable

Navigate privacy concerns, accuracy issues, bias, ethics, and responsible AI use

Duration: 15 min

What You'll Learn

🎓 Knowledge

Understand what AI can and can't do, its limitations and capabilities, and why it matters for your teaching.

💡 Skills

Master practical prompting techniques and learn to evaluate AI output critically using the 5-step critique framework.

🤝 Confidence

Gain hands-on experience in a supportive environment where all questions are welcome and your expertise is valued.

🏛️ Assessment

Explore how to design assessments that integrate—or resist—AI, connecting to the morning assessment design session.
