AI for Professional Staff

Practical guides for using AI in your role—data analysis, compliance, operations, strategic planning, and stakeholder communication.

Explore Professional Staff Guide

This 2-hour interactive workshop is designed for educators with any level of AI experience—from complete beginners to active experimenters. Our goal is AI literacy through hands-on practice, not technical expertise.

For professional staff: While this workshop focuses on teaching, the frameworks and techniques apply equally to analysis, planning, and operations. The Professional Staff resources above provide discipline-specific guidance for your role.

Quick Note: We recognize concerns about AI in education. This workshop addresses them directly. The uniquely human skills of empathy, mentorship, and creative problem-solving become more valuable as we integrate AI, not less.
