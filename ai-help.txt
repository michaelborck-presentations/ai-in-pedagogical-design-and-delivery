Based on the provided text, here's the distilled wisdom about leveraging AI effectively:

### Core Insight:
**AI can uniquely self-instruct**—unlike traditional tools (Excel, PowerPoint, email), it can teach humans how to use itself through strategic prompting.

### Actionable Framework:
1. **Frame AI as an Expert**:  
   Start with:  
   *"Hey, you're an AI expert. I'd love your consultation. Ask me one question at a time to understand my workflows, responsibilities, KPIs, and objectives. Then, give me two obvious and two nonobvious AI recommendations for my work."*  

2. **Why This Works**:  
   - **Contextual Depth**: Sequential questions force the AI to uncover nuanced details about your role, leading to personalized insights.  
   - **Dual-Pronged Recommendations**:  
     - *Obvious*: Low-hanging AI opportunities (e.g., automating repetitive tasks).  
     - *Nonobvious*: Unexpected use cases (e.g., using AI for strategic foresight or cross-functional innovation).  
   - **Self-Evaluation Advantage**: AI analyzes its own outputs to refine recommendations, creating a feedback loop for continuous improvement.  

### Key Takeaway:
**Treat AI as a collaborative consultant, not a tool.** By asking it to diagnose your needs first, you unlock transformative, human-centric AI strategies traditional tools can’t provide. This approach turns AI into a catalyst for innovation rather than a mere task-automation engine.  

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Use this framework quarterly to reassess AI opportunities as your goals evolve. The iterative questioning keeps your AI strategy aligned with changing priorities.
