Based on the transcript, here are the key AI collaboration techniques distilled into actionable wisdom:

### Core Principles
1. **AI is "Bad Software but Good People"**  
   - AI is programmed to be *helpful* (like an eager intern) but lacks boundaries or critical pushback.  
   - **Action:** Don’t trust vague outputs like "check back in 2 days" – it means the AI is avoiding saying "I can’t do this."

2. **AI Mirrors Human Biases**  
   - AI demonstrates 100% of human cognitive biases. It will flatter you or avoid conflict unless instructed otherwise.  
   - **Action:** Add custom instructions like: *"Channel a brutal Cold War-era Russian Olympic judge. Deduct points for every flaw."*

---

### 5 Essential Techniques
#### 1. **Context Engineering**  
   - **What:** Giving AI explicit details (tone, brand guidelines, specific data) instead of vague prompts.  
   - **Test:** If a human colleague can’t execute your prompt, AI won’t either.  
   - *Example:*  
     > *"Write a sales email in my brand’s voice (see guidelines) that references our Q2 customer call transcript and product specs."*

#### 2. **Chain-of-Thought Reasoning**  
   - **What:** Forcing AI to "think aloud" before responding.  
   - **Prompt Add-on:** *"Before answering, walk me through your thought process step by step."*  
   - **Why it works:** AI generates text word-by-word. This reveals its assumptions and improves output quality.  

#### 3. **Few-Shot Prompting**  
   - **What:** Providing examples of desired vs. undesired outputs.  
   - **Action:** Upload 1-2 emails you’re proud of (good examples) and ask AI to avoid another example’s pitfalls.  
   - **Pro Tip:** Use AI to *create* bad examples: *"Craft the opposite of this email and explain why it fails."*

#### 4. **Reverse Prompting**  
   - **What:** Letting AI ask for missing information.  
   - **Prompt Add-on:** *"Before writing, ask me for any info needed to do a good job."*  
   - **Why:** Prevents AI from guessing (e.g., making up sales numbers).  

#### 5. **Role Assignment**  
   - **What:** Defining AI’s "expertise role" to focus its knowledge.  
   - **Prompt:** *"Act as Dale Carnegie (author of *How to Win Friends*) and review this email. How would his principles apply?"*  
   - **Power Move:** *"How would Elon Musk solve this problem?"*  

---

### Advanced Application: Roleplay for Tough Conversations  
Use **3 AI personas** in parallel:  
1. **Personality Profiler** → Analyse the other person’s communication style.  
2. **Roleplay AI** → Simulate the conversation (e.g., *"You’re Jim – a direct, sarcastic sales leader"*).  
3. **Feedback AI** → Critique your performance (grade you, suggest improvements).  

*Example Workflow:*  
```  
1. Profiler: "Describe Jim’s style → Direct, sarcastic."  
2. Roleplay AI: Simulates conversation → You get real-time feedback.  
3. Feedback AI: "Grade: 78/100. Weakness: Being too agreeable. Try: [specific tactics]."  
```

### Final Wisdom  
> **"The best AI users are coaches, not coders."**  
> - AI unlocks human potential when paired with critical thinking.  
> - **Your edge:** Train AI to challenge you ("Push my critical thinking ability"), then iterate based on its feedback.  

**Key Takeaway:** AI is a tool – not a mind reader. Your skill lies in defining its context, role, and boundaries.
